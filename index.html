How it works

Participation has rules.

Scheduled events define who is expected to participate. Torn data verifies activity. Approved leave removes legitimate absences. Extra effort earns more credit.

01

Chains

Orcs and Flamingos have assigned chain days. Members are expected to participate on their own team's scheduled chains. Joining the other team's chain is optional and rewarded.

Competition Principles

What the system is trying to reward.

Showing up beats stat advantage.

Hits and event attendance carry more participation weight than respect efficiency. High stats should not automatically win a participation contest.

Approved leave is not failure.

Jobs, families, school, health, and real life exist. Communicate with leadership and legitimate absences can be excluded from required-event penalties.

Manual Merits require a reason.

Leadership can recognize work the Torn API cannot see, but every manual award should be recorded in the Merit Ledger for transparency.

Be the FLAW that shatters the realm.

Fight when UFF fights. Chain when your team chains. Stay in your OCs. Help each other. Earn Merits all year and make Christmas expensive for leadership.
