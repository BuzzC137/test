Participation Rules

Chains

Orcs and Flamingos have assigned chain days. Members are expected to participate on their own team's scheduled chains. Joining the other team's chain is optional and rewarded.

  • Assigned chain minimum: 18 hits
  • 150+ in one chain: automatic Merit
  • Other-team chains: bonus participation

Wars

UFF generally runs one to two ranked wars each month. If you are expected to fight, the minimum is 30 hits. Approved leave removes the penalty.

  • Minimum: 30 hits per war
  • 150+ in one war: automatic Merit
  • Missed required war: major participation penalty

Organized Crime

Always be in an OC. Required items are provided by the faction where applicable. Reliability matters as much as raw volume.

  • Monthly target: 6+ completed
  • Zero missed assignments: bonus Merit
  • Consistent OC activity: additional credit

UFF Championship Notes

Showing up beats stat advantage. Hits and event attendance carry more participation weight than respect efficiency. Approved leave is not failure. Manual Merits require a recorded reason so the annual contest stays transparent.
